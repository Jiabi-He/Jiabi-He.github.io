I am interested in using mixed methods to explore how cultural capital, meritocratic beliefs, and education markets shape elite college admissions in national and transnational higher education contexts.
- Software: RStudio, Python, NVivo, Qualtrics
- Qualitative methods: content analysis, Foucauldian discourse analysis, critical discourse analysis, grounded theory
- Computational text analysis methods: BERTopic, LDA topic modelling
- Quantitative methods: generalized linear models, factor analysis
